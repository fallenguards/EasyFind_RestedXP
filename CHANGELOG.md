# Changelog

All notable changes to EasyFind: RestedXP Extension are documented in this file.

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-17

### Added
- **The GPS follows your guide**: each waypoint and the next few after it as one route, by walks, lifts, flights and boats, moving on as the guide does; a route you start yourself holds until the guide moves on
- **The step's NPC** on the Target button as you get close
- **RestedXP's arrow** hidden while the GPS leads, unless you keep it
- **Guides in search**: a click loads one
- **The guide's steps in search** and as a list (@rxp): a click jumps the guide there
- **Quests name their guide step**, with "Go to step" on their right-click menu
- **Bag items a later step still needs** are marked
