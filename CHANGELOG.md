# Changelog

All notable changes to EasyFind: RestedXP Extension are documented in this file.

---

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added
- **Items to use as icons**: the items, spells and macros a step says to use sit under the arrow as icons in one box, each with its tooltip and cooldown. Items to use as icons, in this extension's settings, puts them back as named buttons
- **Two targets to a row**: the Target buttons pair up where two fit side by side. Two targets to a row, in the settings, gives each its own row again

Both need EasyFind: GPS Navigation Extension 1.10.0

---

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-21

### Fixed
- **Death skips on Zephras Isle**: on a step that says to die and respawn, your ghost is led to the Spirit Healer the step names, not back to your corpse. Needs EasyFind: GPS Navigation Extension 1.9.1

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-21

### Added
- **The GPS follows your guide**: each waypoint and the next few after it as one route, by walks, lifts, flights and boats, moving on as the guide does, in a fight too; a route you start yourself holds until the guide moves on
- **Travel the guide's way**: the Hearthstone and flight paths are used only on the steps where the guide says to hearth or fly
- **Glide steps**: where the guide says to jump off and use Walk on Air, the GPS points straight at the waypoint, not down the walk around the mountain
- **The step's creatures under the arrow**: each one a step names gets a Target button, in place of RestedXP's Active Targets panel
- **The step's items under the arrow too**: an item, spell or macro a step says to use gets a button there, in place of RestedXP's Active Items panel
- **One arrow**: RestedXP's arrow is hidden while the GPS has a route. A setting brings it back on your own routes, another keeps it always
- **Death skips**: on a step that says to die and respawn, the GPS leads your ghost to the Spirit Healer
- **Grind steps**: the box under the arrow has your XP toward the step's goal
- **Farm circuits**: a step that loops through spots draws its loop on the world map and the minimap
- **Guides in search**: a click loads one
- **The guide's steps in search** and as a list (@rxp): a click jumps the guide there
- **Quests name their guide step**, with "Go to step" on their right-click menu
- **Bag items a later step still needs** are marked
